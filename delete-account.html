Delete Your Ebotola Account

You can request deletion of your Ebotola account and associated cloud data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the Google account associated with your Ebotola account to itzmouhb@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Ebotola Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the Google email address used to sign in to Ebotola.

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete account-associated cloud data that is under our control, including the user's Ebotola account reference and tournament data stored for that account where applicable.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained only where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or resolving disputes. Data processed independently by third-party services such as Google may be subject to their own retention policies.

Processing time

We aim to process valid deletion requests within 30 days.
