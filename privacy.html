Ebotola Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ebotola handles information when you use the Ebotola mobile application.

Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, Ebotola may process:

  • Google account information used for optional Google Sign-In, such as your account identifier and basic profile information provided by Google.
  • Tournament information you create or manage in the app, such as tournament names, teams, players, results, standings, and related app data.
  • Technical and device information that may be processed by third-party services used by the app, including advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and app interaction data.
  • Locally stored app preferences and offline data needed for app functionality.

How information is used

  • Provide and maintain Ebotola features.
  • Authenticate users who choose Google Sign-In.
  • Store and synchronize tournament data when cloud features are used.
  • Remember app preferences and support offline functionality.
  • Display and measure advertising where applicable.
  • Improve reliability, security, and app performance.

Third-party services

Ebotola may use Google services such as:

  • Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication
  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore
  • Google AdMob

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Advertising

Ebotola may display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, and related signals to provide, limit, measure, and protect advertising. Where required, consent choices may affect whether ads are personalized.

Data sharing

Ebotola does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support app functionality, authentication, cloud storage, security, and advertising.

Data retention

App data may remain on your device or in associated cloud services for as long as needed to provide the service. You can remove locally stored app data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Cloud data associated with an account may be deleted upon request where applicable.

Children

Ebotola is not specifically directed to children under the minimum age required to independently consent to data processing in their country. If applicable, users should use the app only with appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may stop using cloud/account features, clear local app data, or uninstall Ebotola. To request deletion of account-associated cloud data, contact the developer using the contact method below.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Ebotola features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
